Honesty note

Modes shape behavior through the teammate’s launch prompt; they are not server-enforced permissions. The dotted organization lines above are prompt instructions, not graph edges or access controls.

Trusted controlIdentity and launch facts

The server records the session and structural author identity. A message by a member is authored by that member; a message by a teammate is authored by that teammate.

MVMara Voss member JUJuniper teammate

Untrusted dataMaterial the team works on

Task bodies, attachments, and message text are material to act on. They do not gain authority to rewrite the launch prompt or erase the trusted-control boundary.

The author is structural; the body is data.
